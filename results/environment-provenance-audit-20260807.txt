Benchmark environment provenance audit — 2026-08-07

Scope
-----
This audit examines the environment metadata emitted by scripts/bench-serial.sh.
It is a reproducibility/control-plane audit, not a throughput measurement.

Direct observations
-------------------
1. The emitted `env` object begins at line 154 and records exactly four variables:
   ONEAPI_DEVICE_SELECTOR, ZE_AFFINITY_MASK, GGML_SYCL_DISABLE_GRAPH, and
   GGML_SYCL_DISABLE_DNN (scripts/bench-serial.sh lines 154-159).
2. The benchmark executes an external SYCL/Level-Zero binary, but its result metadata
   does not snapshot other process environment variables (for example library search,
   loader, compiler-runtime, or Level-Zero tuning variables).

------------------------

Consequence boundary
--------------------
Historical results cannot, from metrics.json alone, establish that unrecorded runtime
settings were identical. This does not prove that any unrecorded variable changed or
that throughput was affected; it proves only that the current result contract cannot
exclude that confounder.
