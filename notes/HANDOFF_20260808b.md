## What landed this session (SHIP_20260808_moe-down-mul-alias.md)
`ggml_sycl_fuse_moe_down_weighted()` hard-rejected fusion when the `mul` tensor
(the weighted per-expert values) overlapped `dst` in the product allocator. The
2026-07-30 regression that motivated the reject was weights/dst alias on other
models; on Laguna decode the alias is the tiny route-weights tensor, and the
`mul` overlap is a scratch-allocator artifact — fusing is safe. Reject now
covers only `weights`/`mmid` (true read-during-write hazards). Result: the
decode down-path weighted-reduce now fuses (`[lx-control-moe-down] fuse hit
(weighted reduce) embd=2048 k=8 tokens=1`), +0.63% decode, KLD-clean, promoted.

The prefill down op (tokens=8) still skips on a genuine weights/dst alias —
leave it, or a future candidate could fix the allocator to avoid that alias.

## CRITICAL finding — the "GPU is down" false alarm
The B70 was never wedged this session. Manual runs that set `LD_LIBRARY_PATH`
to only compiler/dnnl/mkl (+build/bin) fail device enumeration with
`No device of requested type available` — the Level-Zero adapter needs
`/opt/intel/oneapi/tcm/1.5/lib` and `/opt/intel/oneapi/umf/1.1/lib` (libumf.so.1).
`scripts/decode-trace.sh` and `scripts/bench-serial.sh` work because they source
setvars/env.sh and only prepend build/bin, preserving the full path.

**Rule: always `source env.sh` (or setvars.sh) and only PREPEND
`build/bin` to `$LD_LIBRARY_PATH`; never construct the path from scratch.**

`export ONEAPI_DEVICE_SELECTOR=level_zero:gpu ZE_AFFINITY_MASK=0`,
`export LD_LIBRARY_PATH="$CAND:${LD_LIBRARY_PATH:-}"`.
